## **Description**

The UNIT DevLab I2C MPU-6050 6-Axis IMU Module combines a three-axis
accelerometer and a three-axis gyroscope. The board routes the MPU-6050 main
and auxiliary I2C buses, interrupt and synchronization signals, and includes
3.3 V regulation and I2C level shifting.

![](hardware/resources/unit_top_v_1_0_0_i2c_mpu6050_6_axis_imu.png){width=5.4in}

### **Applications**

- Evaluation of acceleration and angular-rate sensing
- Motion-sensing prototypes
- I2C sensor integration and educational experiments
- Experiments using interrupt, synchronization, and auxiliary-sensor signals

### **Hardware Features**

- MPU-6050 three-axis accelerometer and three-axis gyroscope
- Six 16-bit ADCs, one for each accelerometer and gyroscope axis
- Selectable accelerometer ranges of ±2 g, ±4 g, ±8 g, and ±16 g
- Selectable gyroscope ranges of ±250, ±500, ±1000, and ±2000 dps
- 1024-byte sensor FIFO and digital-output temperature sensor
- Main I2C bus on two board connectors and an edge connection
- Auxiliary I2C bus on two board connectors and an edge connection
- `INT`, `AD0`, `FSYNC`, and `CLKIN` signals
- AP2112K-3.3 fixed 3.3 V regulator
- Two BSS138AKDW dual N-channel MOSFET arrays for I2C level shifting
- Red power indicator
- Four mounting holes and ten edge connections shown by the board artwork
