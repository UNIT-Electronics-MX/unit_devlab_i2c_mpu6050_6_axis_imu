### **9.1 Source Hierarchy**

This product reference uses the MPU-6000 and MPU-6050 Product Specification,
Revision 3.4 for sensor characteristics, the released schematic for circuit
connectivity, the manufacturing BOM for fitted component identities and
values, and the released pinout, topology, board views, and dimensioned
drawing for visible labels and physical features. A future approved
validation report takes precedence if it conflicts with this edition.
